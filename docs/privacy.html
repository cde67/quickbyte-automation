Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

QuickByte publishes short tech-tip videos on YouTube and lists them on this website. This page explains what information the QuickByte website and the "QuickByte Automation" app handle. The short version: we don't collect personal information from visitors.

This website

  • It is a static page. There are no accounts, forms, comments, or sign-ups.
  • It does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts.
  • Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability. We do not access or use these logs to identify visitors.

Links to other sites

Pages may link to YouTube and to product pages on Amazon. Some of those links are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

The QuickByte Automation app (Google / YouTube API use)

QuickByte Automation is a private tool used only by the owner of the QuickByte YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission for a single purpose: uploading the channel owner's own videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.

  • It does not read, collect, store, or share data belonging to any other Google user.
  • The channel owner's authorization token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared or sold.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or for any purpose other than uploading the owner's videos.

QuickByte Automation's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do. Questions can be sent through the QuickByte channel on YouTube.
